Original content: Marketing: AEO for SEO: How to maintain search visibility as buyers move to answer engines

Published on Tue, 06 Oct 2026 12:00:03 GMT

Search engine optimization (SEO) teams have long used rankings, organic traffic, and conversions to understand how people find and engage with content. Those signals still matter, but they don’t show whether a brand appears in AI-generated answers.

Buyers can also use ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity to research software, compare vendors, and identify category leaders. When an answer engine summarizes the results, a buyer may not reach a traditional search results page.

Answer engine optimization (AEO) adds another layer of measurement and optimization. SEO teams can use HubSpot AEO to track visibility and citations across answer engines and act on recommendations informed by citation patterns. HubSpot’s traffic-source properties can then help teams evaluate contacts and associated deals whose source is classified as AI Referrals.

Before you optimize for answer engines, establish a baseline: which engines mention your brand, which tracked prompts surface it, how its visibility compares with competitors, and which sources the engines cite.

Traditional search metrics cannot answer those questions. For instance, a page can rank well in Google while a competitor appears more often in AI answers for the prompts you track. Looking at both datasets gives you a fuller view of search visibility.

HubSpot AEO reports a brand visibility score: the percentage of analyzed answers across tracked prompts in which your brand appears. Prompt tracking lets you review the exact responses returned by ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity. Citation reports show which pages and domains the engines reference.

HubSpot runs tracked prompts daily, so review results across multiple days or weeks before evaluating a trend or drawing conclusions. Once you have a baseline, use citation data to decide which content to create or update.

Answer engines cite content from several source types, including owned sites, competitors, industry publications, social platforms, and user-generated content. Looking at those patterns helps SEO teams see which domains, pages, channels, and content types influence answers for their tracked prompts.

For a team with a large content library, checking every prompt and answer engine manually can be slow and inconsistent. A centralized citation view makes it easier to compare pages that perform well in traditional search with pages that appear in AI answers.

In HubSpot AEO, the Citations tab shows top domains and pages, compares owned citation rates with competitors, and groups citations by content type and channel. The Recommendations tab uses citation patterns and site-audit findings to suggest prioritized actions, such as creating or updating owned content, publishing social content, or pursuing outreach.

Use those recommendations to prioritize work, then monitor citation and visibility trends across multiple analysis cycles. Next, compare AI referral outcomes with other traffic sources.

AEO visibility and citation metrics show how your brand appears in AI answers. On their own, they don't show whether that visibility contributes to leads or pipeline. To evaluate business outcomes, pair AEO reporting with HubSpot’s traffic-source and CRM data.

HubSpot categorizes visits from recognized AI platforms as AI Referrals in the Original Traffic Source and Latest Traffic Source properties. The first traffic-source drill-down property identifies the referring AI platform. Associated companies and deals mirror their contacts' source properties, so teams can filter records or build reports to compare AI-referred contacts and deals with other sources.

Treat these datasets as complementary. AEO reports on tracked prompts, visibility, and citations. Traffic-source properties record visits that click through from recognized AI platforms. Because the tools measure different events, don’t treat an AEO mention or citation alone as proof that it caused a particular deal.

HubSpot internal data shows that AEO customers generate 2.6x more leads. Use that benchmark as context, then track whether gains in your visibility and citation coverage coincide with more AI-referred contacts and deals in your own account.

SEO teams should build AEO into their practice rather than treat it as a one-time response to changes in search. Establishing baselines and measurement now gives teams the evidence they need when leadership stops asking “Should we invest in AEO?” and starts asking “Why didn’t we start sooner?”
