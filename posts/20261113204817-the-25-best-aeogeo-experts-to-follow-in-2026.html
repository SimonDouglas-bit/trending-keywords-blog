How have click-through rates changed since AI Overviews appeared? Which websites does ChatGPT cite most often? How do query fanout, RAG, and other AI search complexities actually work?

Research is how you answer these questions, and how you keep up with what's changing in AI search. These are the 25 people publishing the most original research on AI search, and the ones worth following.

I started with a longlist of 87 names: people widely recommended as AEO and GEO experts, and researchers at AI visibility companies.

Then I counted how many original AI search studies each person has published. I pulled every page published on each person's main publishing sites since 2023 (more than 17,000 pages), checked each one against the criteria below, and checked who was credited. Where the same study appeared in more than one place, such as a cross-post, translation or update, I counted it once.
