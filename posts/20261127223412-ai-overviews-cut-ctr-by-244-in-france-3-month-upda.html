On July 22, 2026, Google rolled out AI Overviews in France. Three months later, we looked at what changed.

In our first study (August 2026), the sites most exposed to AI Overviews had lost 23.1% of their click-through rate (CTR) in 9 days.

As the share of AI Overviews kept growing in France, and their impact with it, it was time, as promised, to come back to it. So here we go.

Above, you can see just how quickly AI Overviews spread across French Google desktop results after their 22 July launch, going from 0% of our tracked keywords in June to 88.5% by 25 November, with the fastest jump (35.4 percentage points) coming in August.

No search intent escapes this fate.

Queries that trigger an AI Overview lose 24.4% of their click-through rate—about a quarter.

Between +2.6% and −22.4%, there's a 25-point gap.

Both groups operate in the same market, over the same period. What separates them is the presence of an AI Overview in the SERP.

So this isn't a seasonality effect: on queries without a Google AI Overview, CTR has actually gained ground.

Sidenote: Why the real loss is 24.4%, not 22.4%

The average CTR of keywords that went on to show an AI Overview fell from 2.53% before the rollout to 1.964% in October, a relative drop of 22.4%

But that figure ignores the underlying trend. Over the same period, the CTR of keywords that never showed an AI Overview rose by 2.6% in relative terms.

Had the AIO keywords followed that trend, their CTR would have reached about 2.596% (2.53% × 1.026). Comparing the observed CTR to this expected CTR gives 1.964 / 2.596 = 0.757, a relative decline of about 24.4%.

In other words, the affected keywords didn't just lose 22.4%: they also missed out on the increase the other keywords enjoyed.

We also measured how click-through rate changed by position between June and September 2026.

No position is spared: all of them lost more than 20% of their CTR in three months.

Even the top spot, which captures the most clicks, isn't protected: its CTR dropped from 11.06% to 8.69%.

On the other hand, the CTR for keywords without AI Overviews rose between June and September (position 1 went from 11.06% to 11.78%).

Against that baseline, the net effect on position 1 keywords with an AIO is actually -26.2%, compared with the -21.4% observed above.

Whatever the search intent, the click-through rate of keywords without an AI Overview went up by 1% (commercial) to 3% (transactional).

And the CTR of keywords with a Google AI Overview went down.

Informational and navigational queries lose about 25% of their click-through rate, net.

Commercial and transactional queries lose about 20%.

In August, we explained that intent mattered more than industry.

That's still true, but intent softens the loss by 4 to 5 points—it doesn't prevent it.

A transactional query that triggers an AI Overview still loses a fifth of its CTR.

You can gauge your exposure in two steps, by finding the queries that trigger an AI Overview and then applying the average CTR drop to the traffic they bring in.

You can use the SERP features filter to see exactly where AI Overviews are triggering for your keyword rankings.

Head to Ahrefs' Site Explorer, navigate to the Organic keywords report, and select AI Overview from the SERP features filter.

For every query where an AI Overview appears, expect about a 25% drop in click-through rate.

For your site, apply that figure to the share of your organic traffic that usually comes from those queries.

If 40% of your traffic used to come from queries that now show an AI Overview, expect to lose around 10% of your organic traffic (40% × 25%), assuming constant impressions.

It's a rough estimate, but it gives you a ballpark figure to act on.

An average isn't a prediction. Some pages hold up better, others drop for reasons that have nothing to do with AI. Use the figure to prioritize, not to forecast.

We compared the CTR of 31,612 french keywords including 7,726 pairs of AIO keywords between June 2026 and October 2026.

Keywords were split into two groups: those that triggered an AI Overview and those that didn't. 

The CTR of keywords without an AI Overview served as the control group: whatever it gained or lost is what would have happened to everyone.

From the control group, we derived an expected CTR without an AI Overview for the affected keywords.

The net effect is the gap between the observed CTR and this expected CTR.

Three months after AI Overviews arrived in France, affected queries lost 24.4% of CTR.

It was a similar story back in August: the sites most exposed to AI Overviews experienced a 23.1% CTR drop in just 9 days.

AIOs are a new step toward zero-click search in France: as soon as an AI Overview appears, click-through rate already drops by nearly a quarter.
