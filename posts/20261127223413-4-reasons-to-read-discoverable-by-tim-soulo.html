Original content: SEO Blog by Ahrefs: 4 Reasons to Read DISCOVERABLE by Tim Soulo

Published on November 18, 2026

Director of Content marketing at Ahrefs Tim Soulo, CMO at Ahrefs, has just published a marketing book called DISCOVERABLE. You should read it. I won’t insult you by pretending this is an unbiased review. Let’s own it: I work at ahrefs! Tim is my boss! I helped edit the book! But I will share my honest take on what you, dear reader, stand to gain from reading DISCOVERABLE. Because marketing today is changing, almost day by day: the way our customers buy, the channels they use, the tools at our disposal, and the way we market with them. You probably feel like you’re running as fast as you can just to stay in the same place. I know I do. To that backdrop, do you need yet another marketing playbook? Another set of competing workflows and tactics? Another loud, inexperienced voice serving their own interest? More complexity and confusion masquerading as advice? I’m being rhetorical: of course not. You need universal marketing principles that work today, worked yesterday, and will work tomorrow. You need guidance from someone who has solved (and continues to solve) the problems you’re working on today. You need useful frameworks and clear ideas, distilled to their simplest essence.

That is DISCOVERABLE in a nutshell. And if my elevator pitch didn’t sell you, maybe these four reasons will:

The question “how are people going to find out that you exist?” is universal. Every business on the planet needs an answer to it.

Tim Soulo, Author, DISCOVERABLE

Discoverable is a collection of fundamental marketing principles, all derived from Tim’s experience leading marketing as Ahrefs scaled to $150 million. Tim is a fantastic marketer, but—dare I say it—he wasn’t always. Once upon a time, he was “just some guy…with a job that was clearly too big for him.” Tim learned marketing not from an MBA course or textbook, but from practical experience: trying a whole bunch of stuff, winning (sometimes) and failing (more often), and learning how marketing actually works in the process. So sure: DISCOVERABLE is full of concrete marketing tactics that you can apply to your business. You'll learn how Ahrefs turned its own team into a distribution network of personal brands, why we let thousands of people abuse a $7 trial for five years, how a "Business Potential Score" replaced funnel thinking in our content calendar, what a $33,115 homepage experiment revealed about getting noticed… and why a company selling marketing data still doesn’t bother with marketing attribution. But you’ll also learn the mindset that underpins it all. This book outlines the thought processes, the epiphanies, the risks (and failures) that helped Ahrefs and Tim claw their way into the big leagues. You will learn better marketing and become a better marketer yourself.

I'd never been a CMO of a tech company before and didn't have a degree from some fancy university that would make me qualified to do it. Hell, even my English was far from perfect! What was I doing here? The guy on Reddit was right. I didn't know what I was doing.

Tim Soulo, Author, DISCOVERABLE

Some books are simple because they are basic, shallow, surface-level. Others are simple because the author has taken great pains to distill decades of knowledge and experience into the core principles that underlie them. DISCOVERABLE is the latter. The book you’re holding is essentially 15 years of doing marketing, studying it, teaching it, and talking about it with hundreds of other professionals, condensed into just a few hours of reading.

Tim Soulo, Author, DISCOVERABLE

And something I’ve learned from working at Ahrefs: Tim is maniacal about simplification. Woe to the unwary marketer who presents a 27-page pitch deck of convoluted theorems and hypotheses. Every part of marketing at Ahrefs runs lean and mean, stripped of all unnecessary processes, complexity, and reporting, and better for it.

I don't want to be arguing with my team about whether a given piece of content is top of the funnel or middle of the funnel. All I want to know is how well we can pitch our product and how likely the reader is to become our customer after reading it.

Tim Soulo, Author, DISCOVERABLE

So the marketing advice in this book is straightforward. There is no jargon or complexity. It requires no MBA. Growing a business and earning mindshare in the market is never easy, but DISCOVERABLE shows that it can be simple. This is not the book you need to understand the latest advances in multi-touch attribution. But this is the book you need if you want to ship more, make better decisions, and find more customers. If you are tired of endlessly justifying your decisions, debating the best way to do things, and you just want to actually go and do some freaking marketing, this book will give you permission. It will give you the language you need to persuade your boss and your teammates too.

You don't learn how to do great work by thinking about it. You learn by shipping it. Stop writing endless strategy documents and trying to plan every move in advance. Just take the first step. Once you're there, the second step will be a lot more obvious.

Tim Soulo, Author, DISCOVERABLE

I read about fifty books a year, and I have discovered one nearly immutable truth: business books suck. That goes double when the books are written by people working at software companies. Usually, “business book” means “collection of blog posts that I read three years ago.” No bueno. DISCOVERABLE is not a blog post that you'll be able to find and read somewhere else. It is a book: labored over, loved (and occasionally hated) by its author, ruthlessly edited and rewritten and refined.

It is packed with unique frameworks, illustrative examples, counterintuitive concepts: Business Potential Score, B.R.E.W., minimum viable effort, second-hand search traffic, firing all cannons, the barbecue guide to SEO.

I judge business books by the number of highlights I make and the number of actionable ideas I can implement right away. And I want you to hold me to that same standard.

Tim Soulo, Author, DISCOVERABLE

And while it's one thing to be a good marketer, it’s another thing entirely to be a good teacher of marketing: someone who can interrogate their own experience to understand why things worked and why they failed, and then parcel up and pass those epiphanies on to others. I know from firsthand experience that Tim is an incredible marketing teacher; read Discoverable, and you will too. (And, like every single facet of Ahrefs marketing, every page reflects Tim's own high standards and obsessive focus on quality… as my DMs can attest to.) Before I was an employee I was an admirer of ahrefs, for one simple reason: they had fun. When most brands are maximally risk-averse, Ahrefs marketing is full of weird and wonderful things: children’s books, selfie battles, sarcastic marketing campaigns, ginormous LED conference screens, memes galore. I have two kids. My shelves at home are bursting with children's bedtime storybooks. I've read hundreds of them over the years, and the stories are always simple. So, I thought, how hard could it be to write one ourselves?

Tim Soulo, Author, DISCOVERABLE

DISCOVERABLE explains where this ethos comes from and how you can justify fun-as-a-marketing-strategy in a world of ROI calculations and quarterly budgets. Better than that, it gives you permission to do the same: it is a manifesto for earning the attention of human beings through the most human marketing tactic of all: being a bit weird, a bit unexpected, and having tons of fun in the process.

While you're still small, go crazy. Whatever silly attention-getting ideas you have, try them. In a few years, with any luck, you'll be bigger, more boring, and more risk-averse. Fun will be harder. So have it now, while you still can.

Tim Soulo, Author, DISCOVERABLE

I’ve learned from Tim the power of listening to intuition and doing weird things that are hard to justify to a CFO. The marketing industry is guilty of treating marketing too much like a hard science, something that can be perfectly quantified and analyzed and optimized. Or as Tim puts it: marketing is not math. It's art.

Tim Soulo, Author, DISCOVERABLE

I’ve become a much better marketer through working with Tim. Read DISCOVERABLE, and you will too.
